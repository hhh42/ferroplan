Self-host Ferroplan for `$ARGUMENTS` without collapsing roles.

1. `/chatman-ecosystem:phase-change` — inspect canonical and effective state.
2. `/chatman-ecosystem:configure` — establish loader, ownership, and bounded modeled conformance.
3. `/chatman-ecosystem:observe` — produce the bounded RDF-shaped world and exactly eight work surfaces.
4. `/chatman-ecosystem:allocate` — run CMCA and bind the allocation envelope; recurse only through a selected parent-receipted node.
5. `/chatman-ecosystem:plan` — retain or produce a deterministic Ferroplan candidate plan.
6. Advance to `actuation=manufacturing` only with a verified envelope.
7. `/chatman-ecosystem:manufacture` — delegate one reversible step to the isolated source manufacturer.
8. Observe the resulting mutation frontier; do not let the old canonical snapshot mask it.
9. Feed admitted drift back into the persistent Session.
10. `/chatman-ecosystem:validate` — exercise exact source, configuration, authority, build, plan, and receipt claims.
11. `/chatman-ecosystem:admit` — bind plan, validator, configuration, allocation, and observation commitments.
12. `/chatman-ecosystem:audit` — replay the chain and compute maximum lawful standing.
13. Continue until the goal is met, bounded search refuses, a dependency blocks, or evidence establishes a typed failure.

Preserve:

- `A = μ(O*)`;
- zero unreceipted actuation;
- one isolated source actuator;
- planner implementation distinct from planning constitution;
- hooks as observation and intent manufacture, not truth;
- generated artifacts as projections of canonical owners.

Do not invoke publication unless the user separately invokes `/chatman-ecosystem:publish` or explicitly requests protected publication. A completed self-host loop does not itself authorize push, merge, or pull-request creation.
