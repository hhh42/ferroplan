# ferroplan 0.23 roadmap — the temporal cycle, and the whole table on one box

Scoped 2026-08-09, the day after the 0.22.0 cut published, from a
fresh nine-way scoping pass: four per-domain decodes of the sixteen
boards (with the 0.21 same-box A/B and every loss named), and five
design reads on the bets two cycles of deferred lists pre-registered
for exactly this moment. 0.22 landed at 58% / 16 boards (2,867/4,916,
373 certified optima); on the thirteen comparable boards it moved
+95 — the floor of its own band — while the three re-entries ran hot
and carried the headline to the stretch. The decode reproduces all of
it to the row.

What makes this cycle different from 0.20–0.22: the three biggest
pots on the fresh table are NOT search-guidance pots. One is a
FEATURE GATE wearing a coverage coat (constraints: 100 of 115
unsolved die at one enforcement site), one is a MEASUREMENT agenda
(the 60 s temporal tier plus the last six ghost boards — and the tier
arithmetic just flipped in our favor), and one is the structural
temporal bet whose probe pair has been pre-registered since 0.22
scoping killed the last accounting lever. The engine work that
remains is follow-through with counted cases, not new swings.

The numbers that order the cycle:

- **The constraints gate: 100 of 115** unsolved rows die at ONE
  rejection site (constraints.rs:545-552 — trajectory constraints on
  durative domains "not yet enforced"), and the mass DECOMPOSES: 11
  instances carry at-end-only constraints (foldable into the goal,
  near-free), 19 carry no timed operator at all (the classical
  monitor compile extends), and the remainder is the real timed
  feature. A correction to the rankings doc rides with this: the
  parser PARSES all four rejected variants — the decline is
  enforcement, not parsing.
- **The temporal tier, re-arithmetic'd on fresh raws: the decay
  FLIPPED.** 0.21's last-doubling ratio was 0.72 (mechanism-first,
  said two cycles of records, correctly). The 0.22 distribution
  reads 20 solves in [7.5,15) and 22 in [15,30) — ratio 1.10, a flat
  tail — projecting ~16–24 of 344 conversions at 60 s, plus it
  retires the turn-and-open churn class that cost 2014-tempo −3 this
  cut (all three losses sat 1.4–3.8 s from the 30 s wall). The tier
  is no longer an anti-pot; it pays, and it pays for the time /
  metric-time re-baseline at the same sitting.
- **493 optimal-proof timeouts**, one shape (487 node-cap notes),
  with two counted machinery cases: incremental LM-cut (7 rows / 5
  distinct instances hold a prior-binary LM-cut certificate 0.22
  near-misses; the 0.22 cert distribution is hard-truncated at
  35.35 s where 0.21's tail reached 59.65 s — the probe either
  certifies fast or dies) and the numeric-optimal fold (numRPG armed
  on every 2026-opt certificate but bought only +1 board row — the

- **One new debt, hatch-invisible: onlycraft −6.** Six former
  17–44 s solves (−sat/−opt in lockstep) died at 0.22 and survive
  EVERY single-phase restore hatch and their combination — the exact
  "budget reallocations are invisible to hatches" class the record
  warns about. The open v0.19/v0.21.0 backfill sweep is the named
  opener; nothing prices 0.23's reallocations without that column.

And the standing discipline: the driver's referee verdict came back
NET POSITIVE with a short honest casualty list (floor-tile-2011 i11

## Phase 2 — the constraints gate opens (a feature phase with a coverage referee)

The one place on the table where 100 instances sit behind a single
`if`. Staged, cheapest first, each stage its own referee:

- **Stage a — the at-end fold (+11 attempts, near-free):** an
  `(at end φ)` trajectory constraint on a durative domain is a goal
  conjunct; fold it and let the temporal path run. storage i1–i10 +
  tpp ×1 are the constituency. Fixture: a minimal durative domain
  with an at-end constraint, RED at today's gate, GREEN folded, VAL
  as the oracle.
- **Stage b — untimed monitors on the temporal path (+19
  attempts):** `always` / `sometime` / `at-most-once` /
  `sometime-before` with no timed operator — the classical monitor
  compile (which already enforces these untimed, and already beats
  SGPlan5 on the preference boards) extends to the snap-compiled
  temporal task. The monitor predicates ride the snap facts; the
  0.18 ε-machinery and the 0.22 topological emission are the
  soundness substrate it waited for.
- **Stage c — the timed operators themselves** (`within`,
  `always-within`, `hold-during`, `hold-after`): the real feature,
  sized honestly during stage b (deadline facts against the
  decision-epoch clock are the natural encoding — the TILs
  machinery is adjacent). Stage c is TAKEN only if stages a+b land
  clean and the sizing stays phase-shaped; otherwise it is the
  named 0.24 centerpiece with two stages of receipts behind it.
- **The rider that comes free:** the same machinery is the
  complex-preferences unlock (IPC-5's last locked track — "last of
  3 until the feature ships" per the rankings doc). Not promised
  this cycle; named so the stage-c sizing counts it.
- Referee: the constraints board (5/120 today, field winner 58.8%
  on the scored subset), engine-reject class → near-zero, VAL green
  on every new solve. Band: +25–45 from stages a+b alone.
