## How to read this

- **coverage** is solved/total at that track's official-ish budget (60 s satisficing, 30 s temporal, 300 s where marked an entry).
- **⚖️ marks a proof track**: coverage is the share of instances PROVEN optimal, a far harder bar than finding some plan.
- **vs previous** compares only against a release measured on the same hardware. A blank means no comparable predecessor exists yet, not zero movement.
- A board is only as honest as its conditions; those are recorded per cycle in `docs/roadmap-0.N.md`.
