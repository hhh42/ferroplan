# IPC-2011 temporal field-results attribution

`ipc2011-talk.pdf` is the official IPC-2011 (Seventh International Planning
Competition, deterministic part) results presentation given at ICAPS-11,
produced by the organizers Carlos Linares López, Sergio Jiménez Celorrio,
and Ángel García Olaya (Universidad Carlos III de Madrid). It contains the
per-track results tables — including the temporal satisficing track heat
tables (pp. 88–98) that are the only surviving official per-domain artifact
for that track.

Vendored 2026-08-07 from the Wayback Machine capture of the original site
(the live host `www.plg.inf.uc3m.es` is DNS-dead and the organizers' svn
server `pleiades.plg.inf.uc3m.es` is gone):

- https://web.archive.org/web/20220221174358/http://www.plg.inf.uc3m.es/ipc2011-deterministic/attachments/Results/ipc2011-talk.pdf

The vendored copy doubles as preservation, in the same spirit as
`benchmarks/IPC5-results.tgz`. The machine-readable official archives —
`ipc2011-results.tar.bz2` (per-domain score tables) and
`ipc2011-snapshots.tar.bz2` (validated per-instance snapshots), both linked
from the site's Results page — were never captured by the Wayback Machine
(CDX-verified 2026-08-07) and no public mirror was found; per-instance
IPC-2011 temporal data must be considered lost unless the organizers
resurface it. The distilled field numbers, with all sources, live in
`benchmarks/ipc2011-temporal-field.md`.
